Inside the workbench

02 / DIAGNOSE FIRST

Eleven sections, one habit

Diagnostics, Repair, Updates, Devices, Network, Apps, Maintenance, Onboarding, Security, BitLocker and Privacy. Every tool reads before it offers anything, and a full system scan ranks what needs attention.

03 / GUARDED REPAIRS

Fix only what is proven

A repair is offered only when fresh evidence matches the exact problem. It needs your confirmation, rechecks everything right before it runs, and reads the result back before it says Verified.

04 / EVIDENCE YOU CAN FOLLOW

No black boxes

Findings link to the event, log line or setting behind them. Incomplete evidence is never reported as healthy, and history stays history. Export the report straight into a ticket.

05 / BITLOCKER AND TPM

Encryption without guesswork

Inspect protectors, back up recovery passwords to a file, Active Directory or Entra ID, encrypt Windows and data drives, turn on auto-unlock and prepare a TPM without ever clearing it.

06 / PRIVACY AND DECLUTTER

Make a laptop yours

Review and change privacy settings, app permissions and cloud search, then declutter Windows. Settings owned by policy or device management are left alone.

07 / INTERRUPTION RECOVERY

Check before retrying

Journaled repairs record a pending request before dispatch. If a request is interrupted, recovery checks what happened without automatically repeating the change.

08 / LOCAL DIAGNOSTICS

No AI required

Diagnostics and report analysis run locally without an AI service or KillerBench account. Actions you select, such as downloads or recovery-key backup to Entra ID, can contact their destination. The same Engine powers the desktop app and command line.
